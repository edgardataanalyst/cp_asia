Our Company

Committed to Quality and Customer Satisfaction.

CP ASIA ENTERPRISES is a family-owned business established in 2016, committed to supporting foodservice professionals by providing high-quality equipment and reliable after-sales service for the food and beverage industry.

We specialize in made-to-order and customized stainless steel kitchen equipment tailored to the specific needs of residential kitchens, hotels, resorts, restaurants, commissaries, convenience stores, factories, milk tea shops, condominium units, and government and private establishments.

Through our commitment to quality fabrication and customer service, we provide kitchen solutions designed around the requirements of every client.

Our Materials

Built with SS 304 Food-Grade Stainless Steel.

Our products are crafted using SS 304 food-grade stainless steel, selected for its durability, anti-corrosion properties, long service life, and clean, professional appearance.
